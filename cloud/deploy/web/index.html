Parts with Friends

Projects of 3D prints that you print with friends: the prints, their time lapses, and photos. Sign in with the passkey of this service.

No passkey yet? The owner makes one on the deck: Settings, Cloud, Owner sign-in. A friend gets an invite link from the owner.

Your owner passkey

This phone or computer saves a passkey for this service. Face ID, Touch ID or the PIN of the device unlocks it. The service keeps no password.

An invite

A passkey works for all the projects that you join. Do you have a passkey for Printdeck from another invite? Tap Join with my passkey. Else, Join with a new passkey saves one on this phone or computer. Face ID, Touch ID or the PIN of the device unlocks it.
