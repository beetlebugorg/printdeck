Connect to Wi-Fi

  1. 1Switch on the Printdeck. The setup opens.
  1. 2Tap your Wi-Fi network, then type its password.
  1. 3Wait until the deck is connected, then tap Next.
  1. 4Write down the address and the password, then tap Finish.

  1. 2Wait until the deck finds the board. If the board is missing, tap Search again.

Install the Klipper firmware

The printer board must run the Klipper firmware of this Printdeck.

  1. 1Open Machine, then Maintenance, then tap Firmware. A new board shows Install Klipper firmware on the setup screen.
  1. 2Tap Write. Keep the printer on until the deck shows the result.
  1. 3Tap OK. Klipper restarts and connects to the board.
Set up

The firmware flash

  1. 1Step. Starting the bootloader, Writing, then Checking.
  2. 2Progress. The part of the firmware that is done.

The deck stops Klipper, writes the firmware, checks it, and starts Klipper again. Printdeck flashes atmega2560 boards with an STK500v2 bootloader, for example RAMBo and RAMPS.

The deck

The deck shows the idle screen when no print runs. A key with an LED is on while its LED is lit. A key with no LED does an action.

The status panel is at the top of each screen. The page keys are at the bottom of the idle screen. Each page has Back at the top and its actions at the bottom.

The deck in a browser

Open http://printdeck.local in a browser on the same network. Log in with the password of the deck. The browser shows the screens of the touch screen. Each browser has its own screens.

On a phone, tap Add to Home Screen. The deck then opens as an app.

The page http://printdeck.local/system shows the version, the logs and the API key for slicers. It also installs an update file, and makes a backup. See Back up your data.

15

Send a file from the slicer

The deck uses the OctoPrint API, so slicers can send files and start prints.

Cura: add the deck with the OctoPrint Connection plugin. The deck asks "Allow Cura to use the Printdeck?". Tap Allow.

OrcaSlicer or PrusaSlicer: add a printer of the type OctoPrint, with the address http://printdeck.local and the API key of the system page.

The webcam is the camera with the use Apps: http://printdeck.local/webcam/stream and /webcam/snapshot, with the API key. Add camera=2 for the second camera, w=320 for smaller pictures, or fps=2 for fewer frames. With no camera, the webcam shows the deck screen.

Make a time lapse

The deck camera captures a picture at each layer change. At the end of the print, the deck makes a video.

For each picture, the printer retracts, lifts the nozzle and moves the head to the left side. A bed that moves in Y goes to the middle of the part, so the bed is at the same place in each frame. On the TAZ Workhorse the nozzle goes to Z 10 mm or more, over the bed washers. Each picture adds a few seconds.

  1. 1Aim the camera: Settings, then Camera.
  1. 2Open Settings, then Time lapse.
  1. 3Check Time lapse at the top of the page to turn it on.
  1. 4With two cameras, open Settings, then Cameras. Tap a camera, and check Time lapse or Pictures.
  1. 5Tap First layer, and choose a picture of the first layer, or a picture and a pause. At a pause, check the layer, then tap Resume.
  1. 6Print. After the first layer, the deck shows its picture.
  1. 7At the end of the print, tap Time lapse on the done screen. The video plays.
  1. 8To see it later, open Machine, then Statistics, then History. Tap the print, then Play.
  1. 9To download the video, open http://printdeck.local/system in a browser. The Time lapse part has the video. Click its name.

Change the filament

Set the loaded filament here, so Preheat uses its temperatures. The deck keeps a list of your rolls. Each print subtracts its filament from the roll in the printer.

  1. 1Open Filament, then tap Change filament.
  1. 2Tap the roll that goes in, or tap New roll and weigh it. The nozzle heats for its material.
  1. 3Tap Unload, and hold the filament lightly while it comes out.
  1. 4Push the new filament into the extruder until it stops, then tap Load.
  1. 5Check that clean plastic of the new color comes out, then tap Done.

Level and calibrate

Maintenance lists the calibrations of your printer. Open it from Machine.

  1. 1Open Machine, then Maintenance.
  1. 2Tap Bed tilt or Bed mesh to probe the bed. The deck shows the action until it ends.
  1. 3Tap Probe Z offset for the paper test. Move the nozzle down until a sheet of paper drags a little, then tap Accept.
  1. 4Tap PID tune after you change a heater or a fan. A PID tune runs for about 10 minutes.
  1. 5Tap Save config to write the results. Klipper restarts.
  1. 6Open Machine, then Calibration. Tap Pressure advance, then Print the tower. After the print, tap Pressure advance, then From the tower, and type the height of the sharpest corners.
  1. 7Tap Retraction, then Print the tower. After the print, tap Retraction, then From the tower, and type the height of the lowest band with no strings.
  1. 8After a change to the probe mount, tap Probe offset, then Print the pad and find it. After the print, tap Probe offset, then Set from the last pad. Then do the twist and the bed map again.
  1. 9To see how the bed changes when it heats, open Maintenance, then Heat change, tap Test, and then Heat sweep. The deck prints a G-code file that maps the bed at room temperature, then each 5 °C from 35 to 60 °C, and shows each map less the cold map. The sweep runs for about 75 minutes, after the bed cools.
Level and calibrate
49
  1. 10The bed changes its shape for many minutes after it gets hot. To measure this, tap Test, and then Drift test. The bed cools to 30 °C, heats to 60 °C, and the probe measures the middle of the bed each minute for 20 minutes. Drift test, boost does the same test, but the bed first heats to 65 °C for 3 or 6 minutes, then goes back to 60 °C. Compare it with the drift test: a boost can make the bed settle sooner. Last drift results shows how far the bed moved, the time to 90 % of the movement, and the time after which the bed stays within 0.03 mm. BED_SETTLE in PRINT_START uses this: it probes the middle of the bed every 30 s, and the print starts when the bed stops moving.
  1. 11Tap Height check, then Print, measure only. After the print, tap Height check, then Last results. Then print it again with Print, measure and correct, and compare the results and the heights of the two towers.
  1. 12To cool the bed before the end of a print, open Maintenance, then Bed cool. Set a hold temperature for each material. The deck sets the bed to the hold when the time left is the time that the bed needs to cool. It learns that time from each cool of the bed. It does not cool the bed before half of the print, or for a part that is tall on a small base.

50

The Maintenance page

  1. 1Bed tilt. Probes the bed and corrects its tilt. A printer with a bed mesh shows Bed mesh.
  2. 2Bed cool. Sets the bed to a hold temperature a little before the end of a print, so the part can come off sooner.
  3. 3Probe Z offset. The paper test for the nozzle height.
  4. 4PID tune. Heats the nozzle to 210° and the bed to 60°, and tunes each heater.
  5. 5Nozzle change. Heats the nozzle to 250° and raises the head.
  6. 6Firmware. Writes the Klipper firmware. Orange text shows old firmware.
  7. 7Simulated printer. Runs a simulated printer. Orange text shows a simulated printer in use.
Level and calibrate
51

The Calibration page

  1. 1Run all. Heats, then runs the Z offset, the twist and the bed map, one after the other.
  2. 2Z offset. The paper test for the nozzle height, hot.
  3. 3Probe offset. Prints a 20 mm pad. The pin then finds the edges of the pad, and the deck finds x_offset and y_offset of the probe.
  4. 4Input shaping. Opens the tests of the input shaper: the ringing tower and the check tower.
  5. 5Pressure advance. Prints the test tower, or sets the value from it. The pressure advance goes up 0.005 for each mm of the tower. Type the height of the sharpest corners.
  6. 6Retraction. Prints the test tower, or sets the length from it or by hand. The length goes up 0.25 mm each 3 mm band. Type the height of the lowest band with no strings.
Level and calibrate
52

The Calibration page (more)

  1. 7Height check. Prints a 20 mm tower with a small pad. After each layer the probe taps the pad. Print it once to measure only, and once with the correction of the Z offset. Last results shows the errors.

Each row opens its choices. A printer with no probe shows only Pressure advance and Retraction.

The retraction is the length of [firmware_retraction]. Turn on Firmware Retraction in the printer settings of the slicer, so the G-code has G10 and G11.

Calibrate the camera

A calibration lets the camera views and the time lapse crop follow the part.

The deck prints 5 towers and you tap them on a picture. A bed that moves front to back goes to two places, so the deck also gets the depth.

  1. 1Open Settings, then Camera, then Calibrate.
  1. 2Tap With a print. Clear the bed, then tap Print. The towers print in the loaded material.
  1. 3After the print, leave the towers on the bed and tap Go on. The toolhead moves to the left side, and the bed moves.
  1. 4For each mark, tap the bottom or the top of the tower, then tap Use mark. Tap Skip for a mark that you cannot see.
  1. 5The bed moves to the front. Tap the top of each tower again.
  1. 6Check that the green lines are on the towers, then tap Save. Remove the towers.

Update the Printdeck

The deck checks for a new version once a day. Machine then shows Software update in orange.

The deck installs only a signed version. If the new system fails to start, the deck goes back to the old one.

  1. 1Open Machine, then Software update.
  1. 2Read the notes of the new version, then tap Install.
  1. 3Wait for the download. With no print, the deck restarts into the new version.
  1. 4During a print, the deck installs the update and the print continues. Machine shows Update ready. The deck restarts into the new version after the print, when the printer is idle and the bed is cool.

Back up your data

A backup is one file with your rolls, history, settings, API keys, paired phones and Klipper config. Make a backup before you change the SD card.

Keep the backup password safe. Without it, no one can restore the backup.

The backup excludes the deck password and the Wi-Fi network.

  1. 1Open http://printdeck.local/system in a browser, and log in with the password of the deck.
  1. 2Under Backup, type a backup password of 8 characters or more, two times. To add the G-code files, select Include the G-code files.
  1. 3Click Download the backup. The browser saves a file with the name printdeck-backup-NAME-DATE.pdbk.
  1. 4To restore, open the same page when no print runs. Under Restore, type the backup password, choose the file, then click Restore.
  1. 5Klipper and the Printdeck restart. A wrong password or a damaged file changes no data.

Try the deck with a simulated printer

The simulated printer heats, homes, probes and prints in real time, and no motor moves. Use it to learn the deck or to test a file.

Your printer.cfg stays as printer.cfg.real until you tap Real printer.

  1. 1Open Machine, then Maintenance, then Simulated printer. The setup screen also has this key.
  1. 2Tap the printer to simulate, then Simulate. Klipper restarts with the simulated printer.
  1. 3Print as on a real printer: send a file from the slicer, or tap Print a file.
  1. 4To stop the simulation, open Machine, then Maintenance, then Simulated printer. Tap Real printer, then Go back. Klipper restarts with your printer.cfg.

Settings

The file printdeck.txt on the PRINTDECK partition holds the settings. Put the SD card in a computer to change it. The deck reads it at each start, and clears the Wi-Fi password in it.

wifi_ssid, wifi_password, wifi_countryThe Wi-Fi network
name, hostnameThe name that slicers show, and the name on the network (hostname.local). Settings, Name on the deck sets both
timezoneThe time zone
display_rotateThe screen turn: 90 or 270 turns a wide display to tall, 0 or 180 keeps the native shape. Empty is 0 for a tall display (the Touch Display 2) and 270 for a wide display
touch_invert_x, touch_invert_y, touch_swap_xy1 turns an axis of the touch screen
preheat_tool, preheat_bedThe temperatures of Preheat with no filament set
night_from, night_toThe hours of the night colors
printer.cfg (a file)A Klipper config. The deck imports it at the next start.
