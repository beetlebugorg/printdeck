Connect to Wi-Fi

  1. 1Switch on the Printdeck. The setup opens.
  1. 2Tap your Wi-Fi network, then type its password.
  1. 3Wait until the deck is connected, then tap Next.
  1. 4Write down the address and the password, then tap Finish.

The setup: step 1

  1. 1Networks. Tap your network, then type its password.
  2. 2Scan. Scans for the networks again.
  3. 3Hidden. For a hidden network: type its name and its password.
  4. 4Next. Goes to the summary when the deck is connected.

4

The setup: step 2

  1. 1Address. The address of the deck. Open it in a browser.
  2. 2Password. The password of the web pages. Machine > Wi-Fi and help shows it again.
  3. 3Finish. Closes the setup. The setup shows only at the first start.

To set the Wi-Fi before the first start, put the SD card in a computer. Fill in printdeck.txt on the PRINTDECK partition.

Choose your printer

A new Printdeck has no printer config, so it shows the printer setup.

  1. 1Connect the printer to the Printdeck with a USB cable, and switch the printer on.
  1. 2Wait until the deck finds the board. If the board is missing, tap Search again.
  1. 3Tap Choose printer. The printers of the brand of your board are at the top.
  1. 4Tap your printer, then Use. Klipper restarts with its config.

Install the Klipper firmware

The printer board must run the Klipper firmware of this Printdeck.

  1. 1Open Machine, then Maintenance, then tap Firmware. A new board shows Install Klipper firmware on the setup screen.
  1. 2Tap Write. Keep the printer on until the deck shows the result.
  1. 3Tap OK. Klipper restarts and connects to the board.
Set up
9

The firmware flash

  1. 1Step. Starting the bootloader, Writing, then Checking.
  2. 2Progress. The part of the firmware that is done.

The deck stops Klipper, writes the firmware, checks it, and starts Klipper again. Printdeck flashes atmega2560 boards with an STK500v2 bootloader, for example RAMBo and RAMPS.

The deck

The deck shows the idle screen when no print runs. A key with an LED is on while its LED is lit. A key with no LED does an action.

The printer display is at the top of each screen. The page keys are at the bottom of the idle screen. Each page has Back at the top and its actions at the bottom.

The deck in a browser

Open http://printdeck.local in a browser on the same network. Log in with the password of the deck. The browser shows the screens of the touch screen. Each browser has its own screens.

On a phone, tap Add to Home Screen. The deck then opens as an app.

The page http://printdeck.local/system shows the version, the logs and the API key for slicers. It also installs an update file, and makes a backup. See Back up your data.

15

Send a file from the slicer

The deck uses the OctoPrint API, so slicers can send files and start prints.

Cura: add the deck with the OctoPrint Connection plugin. The deck asks "Allow Cura to use the Printdeck?". Tap Allow.

OrcaSlicer or PrusaSlicer: add a printer of the type OctoPrint, with the address http://printdeck.local and the API key of the system page.

The webcam is the camera with the use Apps: http://printdeck.local/webcam/stream and /webcam/snapshot, with the API key. Add camera=2 for the second camera, w=320 for smaller pictures, or fps=2 for fewer frames. With no camera, the webcam shows the deck screen.

33

Make a time lapse

The deck camera captures a picture at each layer change. At the end of the print, the deck makes a video.

For each picture, the printer retracts, lifts the nozzle and moves the head to the left side. The bed stays where it is, under the camera. On the TAZ Workhorse the nozzle goes to Z 10 mm or more, over the bed washers. Each picture adds a few seconds.

  1. 1Aim the camera: Settings, then Camera.
  1. 2Open Settings, then Time lapse.
  1. 3Tap Time lapse to turn it on.
  1. 4With two cameras, open Settings, then Cameras. Tap a camera, and check Time lapse or Pictures.
  1. 5Tap First layer for a picture of the first layer, or a picture and a pause. At a pause, check the layer, then tap Resume.
  1. 6Print. After the first layer, the deck shows its picture.
  1. 7At the end of the print, tap Time lapse on the done screen. The video plays.
  1. 8To see it later, open Machine, then Statistics, then History. Tap the print, then Play.
  1. 9To download the video, open http://printdeck.local/system in a browser. The Time lapse part has the video. Click its name.

Change the filament

Set the loaded filament here, so Preheat uses its temperatures. The deck keeps a list of your rolls. Each print subtracts its filament from the roll in the printer.

  1. 1Open Filament, then tap Change filament.
  1. 2Tap the roll that goes in, or tap New roll and weigh it. The nozzle heats for its material.
  1. 3Tap Unload, and hold the filament lightly while it comes out.
  1. 4Push the new filament into the extruder until it stops, then tap Load.
  1. 5Check that clean plastic of the new color comes out, then tap Done.

Level and calibrate

Maintenance lists the calibrations of your printer. Open it from Machine.

  1. 1Open Machine, then Maintenance.
  1. 2Tap Bed tilt or Bed mesh to probe the bed. The deck shows the action until it ends.
  1. 3Tap Probe Z offset for the paper test. Move the nozzle down until a sheet of paper drags a little, then tap Accept.
  1. 4Tap PID tune after you change a heater or a fan. A PID tune runs for about 10 minutes.
  1. 5Tap Save config to write the results. Klipper restarts.
  1. 6Tap Pressure advance test to print the tower. Then tap Pressure advance, and type the height of its sharpest corners.
Level and calibrate
48

The Maintenance page

  1. 1Bed tilt. Probes the bed and corrects its tilt. A printer with a bed mesh shows Bed mesh.
  2. 2Probe Z offset. The paper test for the nozzle height.
  3. 3PID tune. Heats the nozzle to 210° and the bed to 60°, and tunes each heater.
  4. 4Nozzle change. Heats the nozzle to 250° and raises the head.
  5. 5Firmware. Writes the Klipper firmware. Orange text shows old firmware.
  6. 6Save config. Writes the results into printer.cfg. Klipper restarts. Orange text shows unsaved results.
  7. 7Pressure advance test. Prints a 30 mm tower. The pressure advance goes up 0.005 for each mm. Measure the height of the sharpest corners.

Update the Printdeck

The deck checks for a new version once a day. Machine then shows Software update in orange.

The deck installs only a signed version. If the new system fails to start, the deck goes back to the old one.

  1. 1Open Machine, then Software update.
  1. 2Read the notes of the new version, then tap Install. The deck installs only when no print runs.
  1. 3Wait for the download. The deck restarts into the new version.

Back up your data

A backup is one file with your rolls, history, settings, API keys, paired phones and Klipper config. Make a backup before you change the SD card.

Keep the backup password safe. Without it, no one can restore the backup.

The backup excludes the deck password and the Wi-Fi network.

  1. 1Open http://printdeck.local/system in a browser, and log in with the password of the deck.
  1. 2Under Backup, type a backup password of 8 characters or more, two times. To add the G-code files, select Include the G-code files.
  1. 3Click Download the backup. The browser saves a file with the name printdeck-backup-NAME-DATE.pdbk.
  1. 4To restore, open the same page when no print runs. Under Restore, type the backup password, choose the file, then click Restore.
  1. 5Klipper and the Printdeck restart. A wrong password or a damaged file changes no data.

Try the deck with a simulated printer

The simulated printer heats, homes, probes and prints in real time, and no motor moves. Use it to learn the deck or to test a file.

Your printer.cfg stays as printer.cfg.real until you tap Real printer.

  1. 1Open Machine, then Simulated printer. The setup screen also has this key.
  1. 2Tap the printer to simulate, then Simulate. Klipper restarts with the simulated printer.
  1. 3Print as on a real printer: send a file from the slicer, or tap Print a file.
  1. 4To stop the simulation, open Machine, then Simulated printer, and tap Real printer, then Go back. Klipper restarts with your printer.cfg.

When something goes wrong

When Klipper stops with an error, the deck shows the message.

  1. 1Read the message. It often names the setting in printer.cfg.
  1. 2After a stop or a power cut of the printer, tap Firmware restart.
  1. 3After a change to printer.cfg, tap Restart Klipper.

Settings

The file printdeck.txt on the PRINTDECK partition holds the settings. Put the SD card in a computer to change it. The deck reads it at each start, and clears the Wi-Fi password in it.

wifi_ssid, wifi_password, wifi_countryThe Wi-Fi network
hostname, timezoneThe name on the network, and the time zone
display_rotateThe screen turn: 90 or 270 turns a wide display to tall, 0 or 180 keeps the native shape. Empty is 0 for a tall display (the Touch Display 2) and 270 for a wide display
touch_invert_x, touch_invert_y, touch_swap_xy1 turns an axis of the touch screen
preheat_tool, preheat_bedThe temperatures of Preheat with no filament set
night_from, night_toThe hours of the night colors
printer.cfg (a file)A Klipper config. The deck imports it at the next start.
