Connect to Wi-Fi

At the first start, the deck makes its password and opens the setup.

  1. 1Switch on the Printdeck. The setup opens.
  1. 2Tap your Wi-Fi network, then type its password.
  1. 3Wait until the deck is connected, then tap Next.
  1. 4Write down the address and the password, then tap Finish.

The setup: step 1

  1. 1Networks. The Wi-Fi networks near the deck, with the signal. A lock shows a network with a password. Tap a network, then type its password.
  2. 2Scan. Looks for the networks again. The list also scans by itself.
  3. 3Hidden. For a network that does not show its name: type the name and the password.
  4. 4Next. Goes to the summary when the deck is connected.
Set up
3

The setup: step 2

  1. 1Address. The address of the deck on your network. Open it in a browser for the web pages.
  2. 2Password. The password of the web pages. To see it again, open Machine > Wi-Fi and help.
  3. 3Finish. Closes the setup. The deck does not open it again.

You can also set the Wi-Fi before the first start: put the disk in a computer, and fill in printdeck.txt on the PRINTDECK partition.

Choose your printer

A new Printdeck has no printer config. The deck then shows the printer setup.

  1. 1Connect the printer to the Printdeck with a USB cable, and switch the printer on.
  1. 2Wait until the deck finds the board. Tap Search again if it does not show.
  1. 3Tap Choose printer. The printers of the brand of your board are at the top.
  1. 4Tap your printer, then Use. Klipper restarts with its config.

No printer yet

  1. 1Board. The board that the deck found on USB, its MCU, and its Klipper firmware.
  2. 2Choose printer. Opens the list of Klipper's example configs. See below.
  3. 3Search again. Looks for the board again, for example after you connect the printer.
  4. 4Your own config. You can also copy your own printer.cfg to the PRINTDECK partition, and start the deck again.
  5. 5Wi-Fi and help. The network, the address and the password of the deck.
  6. 6Software update. Installs a new version of Printdeck OS. A new version can fix the problem.

Choose your printer

  2. 2Printer. The brand, the model, the year and the chip. Tap your printer. The deck writes its config as printer.cfg, with the port of your board, and restarts Klipper. The old printer.cfg stays as printer.cfg.bak.
  3. 3Show all printers. Shows the printers for all chips.

Install the Klipper firmware

The board of the printer must run the Klipper firmware of this Printdeck. The deck writes it through the USB cable.

  1. 1Open Maintenance, then tap Firmware. A new board shows Install Klipper firmware on the setup screen.
  1. 2Tap Write. Do not switch off the printer until the deck shows the result.
  1. 3Tap OK. Klipper restarts and connects to the board.

The firmware flash

  1. 1Step. Starting the bootloader, Writing, then Checking.
  2. 2Progress. The part of the firmware that is done.

The deck stops Klipper, resets the board into its bootloader, writes the firmware, and reads it back to check it. Then Klipper starts again. The setup screen and the Klipper error screen also show this key when the board has no Klipper firmware or old firmware. Printdeck can flash boards with an atmega2560 and an STK500v2 bootloader now, for example the RAMBo and RAMPS boards.

The deck

Printdeck is a touch screen for a 3D printer that runs Klipper. It shows the print, and it controls the printer.

When the printer does not print, the deck shows the idle screen. The largest key is the action that you need most: Print a file.

The keys are the keys of a machine. A key with an LED has two states, and its LED is lit while it is on: the page that is open, the material in the printer, Preheat. A key with no LED does an action: an orange ring shows that it took the tap.

Each screen has the same places. At the left, the display of the printer, and under it the key panel: Main (the idle screen), Files, Filament, Move, Maintenance and Machine. At the right, the page that is open: its title and Back at the top, and its actions in one row at the bottom, with the main action at the right.

Meet your Printdeck
8

The idle screen

  1. 1Nozzle. The nozzle now, and its target under it while the heater is on. The icon is filled while the heater is on, and waves rise while it heats. Tap it to set the temperature.
  2. 2Bed. The same for the bed.
  3. 3Fan. The part cooling fan. Its blades turn at its speed.
  4. 4Status. The position of the head, the state of the printer with the loaded filament, and the clock. This is the status screen of the display of the printer: the same picture on both screens.
  5. 5Print a file. Opens the files. See Files.
  6. 6Preheat. Heats the nozzle and the bed for the loaded filament. Its LED is lit while the heaters hold those temperatures: tap it again to turn them off.
  7. 7Cool down. Turns off both heaters.
  8. 8Last print. The last print and its result. Reprint starts it again, after a question.
  9. 9Pages. The key panel: Main (this screen), Files, Filament, Move, Maintenance and Machine. The LED of the page that is open is lit. Each page has its own section.
Meet your Printdeck
9

The display of the printer

When the printer has a display, the deck draws it. The display shows the status screen, the same picture as the idle screen of the deck.

Turn or click the knob to open the menu. The menu has the same pages and keys as the touch screen: the knob moves the cursor, and a click taps the key. The touch screen shows the cursor with an orange ring. The top item goes back: Status on the home menu, Back on a page. A long click also goes back. With no turn for 20 seconds, the display shows the status screen again.

While an action runs, the display shows an animation.

The deck in a browser

Open http://printdeck.local in a browser on the same network, and log in with the password of the deck. The browser shows the same screens as the touch screen, sharp at the pixel density of the phone. Each browser has its own screens, so a phone can show the files while the deck shows the print.

On a phone, use Add to Home Screen: the deck then opens as an app, with no browser bars.

The page http://printdeck.local/system shows the version, the services, the logs and the API key for slicers. It also installs an update: choose the update file, then Install. The deck writes the other system slot and restarts into it. If the new system does not start, the deck goes back to the old one.

Send a file from the slicer

The deck answers the OctoPrint API that slicers use to send files and to start prints.

In Cura, add the deck with the OctoPrint Connection plugin. The deck asks "Allow Cura to use the Printdeck?". Tap Allow.

In OrcaSlicer or PrusaSlicer, add a printer of the type OctoPrint with the address http://printdeck.local, and the API key of the system page.

Start a print

The files are the G-code files that the slicer sent, or that you uploaded in the browser. The newest file is at the top.

Each file shows what the slicer wrote into it: the print time, the filament and the layer height. Cura, PrusaSlicer, OrcaSlicer and Bambu Studio write this data. A thumbnail shows when the slicer adds one.

  1. 1Tap Print a file on the idle screen.
  1. 2Tap the file. Check the print time, the filament and the temperatures.
  1. 3Tap Print, then Print again.

The file list

  1. 1Count. The number of files.
  2. 2Picture. The thumbnail of the slicer, or the shape of a part.
  3. 3File. The name, the print time, the filament and the layer height.
  4. 4Date. When the file arrived.

Drag the list up and down. Tap a file to open it.

A file

14

Watch the print

During a print, the deck shows the screen of the phase of the print. The deck knows the phase from the printer and from the G-code of the file.

  1. 1Watch the first layer. Use − and + to set the height of the nozzle while it prints.
  1. 2Tap Save to keep the Z offset for the next prints.
  1. 3Use Tune to change the speed, the flow or the fan while the print runs.
  1. 4When the print is done, wait for the bed to cool, then take the part off.

Preparing

  1. 1Display. The status screen of the printer display: the nozzle, the bed and the fan, the progress, the time left, and the time on the clock when the print ends. Tap the nozzle or the bed to set its temperature.
  2. 2Steps. The steps of the start macro of your printer, in its order. The deck follows the commands that the printer runs: the step that runs has a lit LED, and a done step has a dot.
  3. 3Help. Opens Wi-Fi and help: the network, the address and the password of the deck.
  4. 4Menu. Shows the other screens while the print runs. See Look around during a print.
  5. 5Cancel print. Stops the print, after a question.
Print a part
15

The first layer

  1. 1Display. The status screen of the printer display: the nozzle, the bed and the fan, the progress, the time left, and the time on the clock when the print ends. Tap the nozzle or the bed to set its temperature.
  2. 2− and +. − moves the nozzle closer to the bed, + away from it. A line that is too round and thin needs −. A line that is flat and rough needs +.
  3. 3Z offset. The height of the nozzle above its normal height. Watch the first lines while they print.
  4. 4Save. Writes the Z offset into the config of the printer. Save config in Maintenance then writes the file.
  5. 5Step. How far one tap moves the nozzle, in mm.
  6. 6Pause, Cancel. Pauses the print, or stops it after a question.
  7. 7Menu. Shows the other screens while the print runs. See Look around during a print.
  8. 8Help. Opens Wi-Fi and help: the network, the address and the password of the deck.
  9. 9Layer. The first layer of the model.

16

Printing

  1. 1Display. The status screen of the printer display: the nozzle, the bed and the fan, the progress, the time left, and the time on the clock when the print ends. Tap the nozzle or the bed to set its temperature.
  2. 2Pause. Pauses the print.
  3. 3Tune. Changes the print while it runs. See Tune.
  4. 4Cancel. Stops the print, after a question.
  5. 5Menu. Shows the other screens while the print runs. See Look around during a print.
  6. 6Model. The model up to the layer that prints now, with the kind of the move now at the top right.
  7. 7Roll. The roll in the printer: a dot in its color, the filament that is left on it, its name, and its grams. The filament of the print counts while it prints. Cura gives a roll its color.
  8. 8Speed. The live speed of the head over the last 30 seconds: a bar for each moment, the newest at the right in orange. At the right, the live speed in mm/s and the flow of plastic out of the nozzle in mm³/s. The bars drop at each corner and rise on long lines of infill.
  9. 9Layer slider. Drag it to look at another layer. Live goes back to the layer that prints.

Tune

  1. 1Help. Opens Wi-Fi and help: the network, the address and the password of the deck.
  2. 2Speed. The speed of the print, in percent of the speed of the slicer.
  3. 3Flow. The amount of plastic, in percent.
  4. 4Z offset. The height of the nozzle above its normal height. Save writes it into the config of the printer, as on the first layer screen.
  5. 5Fan. The part cooling fan.

Look around during a print

Menu on the screens of the print shows the other screens while the print runs: the files, the filament and the rolls, the history. They are read only for the printer, so a tap cannot change the print by mistake.

The print bar at the bottom of each screen shows the print. Tap it to go back. The deck also goes back to the print at a pause, at a new print, and after 2 minutes with no touch.

Filament during a print

  1. 1Print bar. The layer and the time left of the print, with Pause at its left. Tap the bar to go back to the screen of the print.
  2. 2Change filament. A key that moves, heats or changes the printer is dim and has a lock. A tap shows Wait until the print ends.
  3. 3Pages. The key panel opens each page, as when the printer does not print.

Change the filament

Set the loaded filament here, so that Preheat uses its temperatures. The guided change heats the nozzle, unloads the old filament and loads the new one.

The deck keeps a list of your rolls of filament. Weigh a new roll with its reel, and the deck subtracts the weight of the empty reel. Each print subtracts the filament that it used from the roll in the printer.

  1. 1Open Filament, then tap Change filament.
  1. 2Tap the roll that goes in, or tap New roll and weigh it. The nozzle heats for its material.
  1. 3Tap Unload, and hold the filament lightly while it comes out.
  1. 4Push the new filament into the extruder until it stops, then tap Load.
  1. 5Check that clean plastic of the new color comes out, then tap Done.
Change the filament
24

The Filament page

  1. 1Rolls. Opens the list of your rolls. On the list, the key stays in its place with its LED lit, and a tap comes back here.
  2. 2Loaded. The roll that is in the printer and the filament on it, or the material and its temperatures.
  3. 3Material. Tap the material that is in the printer. The deck asks before it changes the loaded material.
  4. 4Change filament. The guided change: choose, heat, unload, insert, load, check. The printer homes while the nozzle heats. Turn this off on the Settings page.
  5. 5Load, Unload. Only the load, or only the unload.
  6. 6Extrude, Retract. Pushes or pulls 10 mm. The nozzle must be at 170° or more.
  7. 7Heat, Cool down. Heats the nozzle for the loaded filament, or turns the heaters off.
Change the filament

The guided change, step 1

  1. 1Step. The step of the change.
  2. 2Rolls. Your rolls. Tap the roll that goes in. The nozzle heats for its material.
  3. 3New roll. Adds a roll: its material, its name, its weight on a scale, the weight of an empty reel, and its price.
  4. 4Other. A filament with no roll: choose the material or type a temperature.
  5. 5Close. Stops the change. After the nozzle starts to heat, Close also turns the nozzle off. During a pause, the nozzle goes back to the temperature of the print.

Move the head and heat

  1. 1Open Move, and tap Home all after each start of the printer.
  1. 2Hold the joystick to move the head. Use Z for the height.
  1. 3Tap a temperature on the idle screen to set it with the keypad.

The Move page

  1. 1Position. X, Y and Z of the head. A lit LED shows a homed axis.
  2. 2Home. Homes all axes, or one axis. Home after each start of the printer.
  3. 3Joystick. Hold a finger on the pad to move the head in X and Y. The farther from the middle, the faster it moves.
  4. 4Z. Moves Z up or down by the step: 0.1, 1 or 10 mm.
  5. 5Park, Center, Motors off. Park moves the head to the back left. Center moves it to the middle of the bed. Motors off lets you move the head by hand. During a pause, Home, Center and Motors off are off, so that the print can continue. Park lifts the nozzle 10 mm before it moves.
Move and heat
29

The keypad of a temperature

  1. 1Value. The temperature that you type.
  2. 2Presets. Off, and the temperatures of the materials.
  3. 3Keys. The digits. C clears the value, DEL removes the last digit.
  4. 4Set. Sets the heater. Cancel closes the keypad with no change.

Level and calibrate

Maintenance lists the calibrations that your printer has. The list comes from the config of the printer.

  1. 1Open Maintenance.
  1. 2Tap Bed tilt or Bed mesh to probe the bed. The deck shows the action until it ends.
  1. 3Tap Probe Z offset for the paper test. Move the nozzle down until a sheet of paper drags a little, then tap Accept.
  1. 4Tap PID tune after you change a heater or a fan. A PID tune runs for about 10 minutes.
  1. 5Tap Save config to write the results. Klipper restarts.
Level and calibrate
31

The Maintenance page

  1. 1Bed tilt. Probes the bed, and corrects its tilt (BED_TILT_CALIBRATE). A printer with a bed mesh shows Bed mesh.
  2. 2Probe Z offset. The paper test for the height of the nozzle (PROBE_CALIBRATE).
  3. 3PID tune. Heats the nozzle to 210° and the bed to 60°, and finds the PID settings of each heater (PID_CALIBRATE). Save config then writes them.
  4. 4Nozzle change. Heats the nozzle to 250° and raises the head, to change the nozzle.
  5. 5Firmware. Writes the Klipper firmware of this Printdeck to the board of the printer. Orange text shows old firmware. See below.
  6. 6Save config. Writes the results of the calibrations into printer.cfg. Klipper restarts. Orange text shows unsaved results.
Level and calibrate
32

An action runs

  1. 1Action. The time since the start, a bar that moves while the action runs, and the temperatures.
  2. 2Stop the printer. Stops the printer at once, after one question. Then use Firmware restart.

While an action runs, the deck shows it on the deck and in each browser, and the other keys are off. The display of the printer shows the name of the action and a moving bar. When the action ends, a short message says that it is done. When it fails, the deck shows the error of Klipper until you tap OK.

The paper test

  1. 1Height. The height of the nozzle now.
  2. 2Steps. Moves the nozzle down (−) or up (+), in mm.
  3. 3Accept. Takes the height. Save config then writes it.
  4. 4Abort. Stops the test with no change.

This screen shows by itself while Klipper runs a paper test, also when it started from a macro.

Look after the printer

Machine has the information of the printer, the history of the prints, the macros, the console and the settings.

The Machine page

  1. 1Information. The version of Klipper, the state of the firmware of the board, the network address, and the totals.
  2. 2Software update. The version of the deck, and a new version when there is one. See Update the Printdeck.
  3. 3Statistics. The totals of all prints.
  4. 4Print history. Each print, with its result. Tap a print to see its model, its time and its filament, and to print it again.
  5. 5Macros. The macros of the printer. Tap one to run it.
  6. 6Restart Klipper. Restarts the Klipper host, which reads printer.cfg again.
  7. 7Firmware restart. Restarts the firmware of the board, for example after a stop.

The Machine page, further down

  1. 1Settings. The theme, the clock, and the homing in a filament change. See below.
  2. 2Wi-Fi and help. The network, the password and the addresses.
Look after the printer
36

The Settings page

  1. 1Theme. The colors of the screen: day, night, or auto (night from 20:00 to 7:00). Tap it to change them.
  2. 2Clock. The clock of the screens and the display of the printer: 12 hours (2:24 PM) or 24 hours (14:24). Tap it to change it.
  3. 3Home in a filament change. On: the printer homes all axes while the nozzle heats for a filament change. A change during a paused print does not home. Tap it to turn it on or off.
  4. 4Screen. The turn of the screen: 0° or 180° for a display that is mounted wide, 90° or 270° for a display that is mounted tall. A tap asks to turn it a quarter turn more, then the deck restarts in the new turn. The touch turns with the screen. A print goes on during the restart. This setting wins over display_rotate in printdeck.txt.

Statistics

  1. 1Totals. The number of prints, the success rate, the print time and the filament.
  2. 2Results. Done (amber), cancelled and failed (red).
  3. 3Details. The filament in grams (for 1.75 mm PLA), the average and the longest print, the prints of the last 7 and 30 days, and the file that you print most.

  3. 3Quick keys. Home (G28), Status (STATUS) and Help (HELP, the list of the commands).

Update the Printdeck

The deck checks for a new version once a day. Machine then shows Software update in orange.

Each version has a signature. The deck installs only a version with a valid signature of Printdeck. It writes the other system slot and restarts into it. If the new system does not start, the deck goes back to the old one.

  1. 1Open Machine, then Software update.
  1. 2Read the notes of the new version, then tap Install. The deck does not install during a print.
  1. 3Wait while the deck downloads and writes the update. It restarts into the new version.

Software update

  1. 1Versions. The version of this deck, the newest version, and the channel.
  2. 2Notes. What the new version changes.
  3. 3Install. Downloads the new version, checks it, and installs it, after a question. The deck restarts.
  4. 4Check now. Checks for a new version now.
  5. 5Beta updates. On: the deck also gets the betas, the versions with new features for tests. Off: only the stable versions.

When something goes wrong

When Klipper stops with an error, the deck shows all the lines of its message.

  1. 1Read the message. It often names the setting in printer.cfg.
  1. 2After a stop or a power cut of the printer, tap Firmware restart.
  1. 3After a change to printer.cfg, tap Restart Klipper.

A Klipper error

  1. 1Message. What Klipper reports, with all its lines.
  2. 2Restart Klipper. Restarts Klipper. It reads printer.cfg again.
  3. 3Wi-Fi and help. The network, the address and the password of the deck.
  4. 4Software update. Installs a new version of Printdeck OS. A new version can fix the problem.

Settings

The file printdeck.txt on the PRINTDECK partition holds the settings. Put the disk in a computer to change it. The deck reads it at each start, then clears the Wi-Fi password in it.

wifi_ssid, wifi_password, wifi_countryThe Wi-Fi network
hostname, timezoneThe name on the network, and the time zone
display_rotate90 or 270 for a display that is mounted tall, 0 or 180 for one that is mounted wide
touch_invert_x, touch_invert_y, touch_swap_xy1 turns an axis of the touch screen
preheat_tool, preheat_bedThe temperatures of Preheat with no filament set
night_from, night_toThe hours of the night colors
printer.cfg (a file)A Klipper config. The deck takes it at the next start.
