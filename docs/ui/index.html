The deck UI

The screens of the deck

Every screen of the touch screen, for a display that is mounted tall or wide.

On this page
  1. The screens
  2. The chapters
  3. The manuals and the booklets

The deck is the application on the 7 inch touch screen. It has two layouts: for a display that is mounted tall, as in the Slate, and for a display that is mounted wide, as on the TAZ Workhorse. The pages of this section show every screen with numbered callouts, in the words of the manual. Each page has a switch between the tall and the wide layout.

The screens

  • The top of each screen shows the display of the printer, with the same dots: the state, the temperatures, the fan, the position of the head, and the progress.
  • The print screen shows a 3D view of the model that prints, in the color of the roll, with the layer, the roll, and the time of each layer above it. It can pause, resume, tune, and cancel. The view is a shaded STL if the upload folder or MODEL_DIR (default /var/lib/printdeck/models) has an STL with a matching name. Otherwise it is the toolpath from the G-code.
  • Toolhead (when no print runs): home X, Y, Z; a joystick moves the head (faster further out, stops when you lift the finger); Z in steps of 0.1, 1, 10 mm; extrude and retract; fan; motors off; emergency stop. A filament swap leads through heat, unload, load, and purge.
  • Tune (while a print runs): speed and flow in percent, Z offset in baby steps, fan.
  • Tap a temperature to set it with a keypad.
  • Machine > Settings > Theme chooses the day theme, the night theme, or auto (night from NIGHT_FROM to NIGHT_TO, 20 to 7). The choice is saved in /var/lib/printdeck/theme.
  • Rolls: the deck keeps a list of your rolls of filament, each with a number and a color. Each print subtracts its filament from the roll in the printer, and the history shows the cost of each print. The page of each roll lists the prints that used it. printdeck.local/rolls/labels prints round labels with a QR code. A phone camera on a label opens the page of the roll, and Load starts the filament change with it.
  • Machine > Maintenance has the calibrations of the printer, the firmware, and the pressure advance test.
  • Machine > Settings > Notifications sends push messages to an ntfy topic: print done, print failed, a pause, the end of the first layer, and a roll that runs short. The ntfy app shows them on the phone and the watch.
  • During a print, the other screens stay open to you. The controls that change the printer are locked. On a wide screen, Main is the print. On a tall screen, the print bar at the bottom goes back to the print.
  • A display that is mounted wide gets the layout of a machine panel: the display of the printer and a key panel at the left, and the page at the right.
  • Setup (at the first start) sets the Wi-Fi. Machine > Wi-Fi and help, and Help during a print, show the address and the password.

slicer/ has a Cura post-processing script that sets the speed of each move. See slicer/README.md. slicer/cura-plugin/ is the Printdeck plugin for Cura: the roll in the printer, its material, and the print in Preview. See slicer/cura-plugin/README.md.

The manuals and the booklets

The manual shows each screen of the deck with numbered callouts, and explains each part. It has two versions, one for each mount of the display:

  • Tall: the web manual, or the booklet docs/manual/printdeck-manual.pdf.
  • Wide: the web manual, or the booklet docs/manual/wide/printdeck-manual.pdf.
  • The upgrade kit for the LulzBot TAZ Workhorse: the booklet docs/lulzbot/printdeck-manual.pdf has the print plan, the hardware and the steps of the mount in lulzbot/, then the wide manual.
  • The Cura plugin: the booklet on the web, or docs/cura/printdeck-cura.pdf. It shows the setup, the Printdeck tab, and the Print on a part tool.

Read the booklets on screen, or print them. The booklet has half-letter pages. A print shop makes a saddle-stitched booklet from printdeck-manual.pdf. To print it yourself, print printdeck-manual-print.pdf on both sides of letter paper (flip on the short edge), fold the stack, and staple it in the fold.

The deck draws the pictures itself: after a change to the UI, run docs/manual/build.sh. After a change to the Cura plugin, run docs/cura/build.sh. The documentation site in docs/ comes from the README files, the guides of the two mounts and the manuals: after a change to one of them, run docs/build.py.

The booklets have the same style as the pictures of this site. The booklet of the Workhorse upgrade has the guides of the mount and then the wide manual.

Printdeck is free software (AGPL-3.0). docs/build.py writes this site from the files of the repository.
