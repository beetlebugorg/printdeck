The two mounts

The Slate

The case is the Slate in slate/: a 35 mm slab that leans back 15 degrees on a foot, with the Pi on the rear. slate/README.md lists the parts and the files. slate/printdeck-slate-build.pdf has the parts list, the print settings, and the assembly steps.

The TAZ Workhorse mount

A second mount puts the Printdeck on the front left of a LulzBot TAZ Workhorse, with the display wide. You print its seven parts on the Workhorse itself: 309 g of PLA in 14 h 58 min, with no supports. Two metal M3 roll-in T-nuts hold the clips in the front rail, and you drill no hole in the printer. The upgrade booklet has the print plan, the hardware and the steps. The files are in lulzbot/.

7 printed parts321 g of PLA15 h 29 minNo supportsNo holes in the printer
