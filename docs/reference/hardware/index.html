The connections

The pod is a Raspberry Pi Touch Display 2 (5 inch, mounted tall) with a Raspberry Pi 5 on its back, in a printed case. A printed bracket holds the pod on the printer: one bracket for the Mini 2, and one for the TAZ Workhorse. lulzbot/README.md has the pod, the brackets and the parts list. lulzbot/stl/pod/README.md has the print files.

What you need

From lulzbot/README.md. The page of the pod has the brackets and the cables on each printer.

  • A Raspberry Pi Touch Display 2, 5 inch. Its box has the DSI ribbon, the power wire and the M2.5 screws.
  • A Raspberry Pi 5 with the Raspberry Pi Active Cooler.
  • An SD card for Printdeck OS. See Install.
  • The Raspberry Pi 27 W USB-C power supply.
  • A USB cable from USB-A to USB-B, from the Pi to the printer.
  • The printed parts and the hardware in stl/pod/README.md. For the TAZ Workhorse, also two rail clips (stl/clip.stl), two M3 x 8 screws and two M3 roll-in T-nuts.
